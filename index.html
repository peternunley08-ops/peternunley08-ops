🕯️ Brain Navigator

A quiet, candlelit iPhone app designed for high school students to keep classes, assignments, focus sessions, and mental clutter from piling up — without streaks, guilt, or scoreboards.

  • Time-aware "Candlelight" UI: Transitions from First Light morning briefings on the Desk tab to a calm Night Room stack after hours.

🏊‍♂️ DeckHand — Swim Roster & Lifeguard Sync

A real-time operations app for summer swim lessons and lifeguard teams. Tracks swimmer attendance across time slots and levels, assigns instructors, and imports paper clipboards by photo.

  • 3-Way Optimistic Concurrency Merge: Poolside instructors can mark attendance simultaneously from multiple phones without overwriting each other's changes.
  • Claude Vision Sheet Import: Reads printed tables and handwritten roster adjustments from a phone camera into a structured review queue.
  • Hardened Security Architecture: Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) and Edge Functions enforce owner-only account creation and wipe local caches on logout.
